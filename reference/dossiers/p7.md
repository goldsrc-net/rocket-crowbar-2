## 7. Behavior worth testing

- RPG: each attack launches its mine, refused at MaxDeployed or short of ammo/energy; the HUD
  counts update 4x a second; empty attacks hold all four timers 0.25 s.
- Satchel: roaches walk in circles, home on the best visible target (current one counts double),
  arm on primary with a RoachArmTime fuse and speed up while arming; water handling (velocity
  scaled by 8*interval under water). Conc charge sticks to world or rides entities, bounces off
  shields, blows when its host dies, pushes everything in ConcBlastRadius (armor damage too) once
  each. Quaternary throws a control_forklift.
- Shotgun: scientist (feet first, scream stops on impact), HEV suit (engine for HEVEngineTime,
  then falls harder), forklift (drives along floors, blows on walls/monsters), crate (charging
  burns shells, then grows until it bursts; damage and radius scale with its size). No fire under
  water without the player flag.
- Shield: deflection point and direction, energy drain and cut-off at MinRunEnergy.
- Scientists heads/pitch, multiplayer corpse fade, no pain sounds while +0x66 is set; snark bites,
  pop damage cap, bolts; mini grunt; snark cam controls (IN_ALT1 release, IN_ATTACK2/IN_CANCEL
  cycle, IN_ATTACK fires for 1 energy); shrink timer (scale to 0.2, crush by players, regrow);
  EMIT_SOUND_DYN clamping.

## 8. Original bugs (all kept)

- RoachThink under water multiplies velocity by 8*interval (a slowdown only for short intervals).
- FindTarget re-uses pev->dmgtime, so an armed roach stops searching and keeps its last target.
- ShieldDeflect passes degrees to cos/sin.
