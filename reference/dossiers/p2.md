# Dossier: work package p2

rc2.dll .text 0x2ae10-0x47092: the crossbow, the crowbar with its crowbarang and rocket, the
death messages, the remote-control forklift, doors, effects, the shrinker and imploder, the egon,
explosions, the freeze timer, breakables and tanks. Written 2026-11-07. The crowbarang/rocket,
crossbow, crowbar, death messages, defaultai and func_tank were done by the package agent; the
forklift/explode/freeze timer, doors/effects/func_break and egon/shrinker slices by three forks
of it working in this worktree, whose reports are merged here.

## 5. Cross-file needs

**The weapon base (merge with p3).** `names-rc2-slots.tsv` mislabels three CBasePlayerWeapon
slots. Read off the bodies: 102 is ResetEmptySound (0xfb380 sets +0x98), 103 SendWeaponAnim
(0xfaf30, which calls slot 113), 113 UseDecrement, and the four RC2 added are 107 and 108 (between
SecondaryAttack and Reload: the third and fourth attacks) and 114, 115 after UseDecrement. 114 is
called first thing by ItemPostFrame (each weapon's per-frame think), 115 takes two pointers and
reads WeaponSync data back (the client's half; on the server its readers return 0). So RC2's
order is 105 Primary, 106 Secondary, 107 Tertiary, 108 Quaternary, 109 Reload, 110 WeaponIdle,
111 RetireWeapon, 112 ShouldWeaponIdle, 113 UseDecrement, 114 WeaponFrame, 115 ReadWeaponSync.
weapons.h's p2 block declares 107, 108, 114, 115 (names ours) and m_flNextTertiaryAttack (+0xb0),
m_flNextQuaternaryAttack (+0xb4): the +8 bytes BASE_VIRTUALS.md mentions. ItemPostFrame (p3) has
to call them.

Shared-class additions (p2 needs blocks):
- CBasePlayer: m_iRC2ControlledEnt (+0x69c, the entity a player acts through: projectiles are
  owned by it), m_iRC2State648, m_iRC2State68c, m_bRC2State10fc (states that bar a tank; meaning
  VERIFY; fork A found +0x648 is a hold count kept by 0x9a800/0x9a820), m_flRC2Energy (+0x758),
